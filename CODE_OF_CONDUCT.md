# Code of Conduct

Weave is a single-maintainer project. Everyone who takes part in it — in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or reviews — is expected to keep the
exchange about the work.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including towards people whose
  first language is not English.
- Criticise code, designs, and decisions; not the people behind them.
- Accept that a contribution can be declined, and explain why when you
  decline one.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to info@werkworks.de. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
anyone from the repository whose behaviour violates this code. Depending on
the severity, a warning comes first; a serious violation can lead to an
immediate block.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of this repository and wherever someone
represents the project publicly.
